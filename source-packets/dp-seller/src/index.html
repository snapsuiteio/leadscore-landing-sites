Before you begin

A few things
you may be wondering.

Does the property need to be renovated first?

The conversation is about selling as-is. Describe the property’s current condition, including any work it needs.

What if I am just exploring my options?

That is one of the choices on the form. Tell us your timing so the conversation can begin with your priorities.

What if the property has tenants?

The form includes owner-occupied, tenant-occupied, vacant and partially occupied options. Select the answer that best describes the property.

Does sending the form mean I have agreed to sell?

No. It is a request to discuss the property. An offer is not guaranteed, and no sale is agreed by submitting the form.
