WHAT YOU’LL ACCOMPLISH

From the work that waits to a workflow you can test.

Three practical examples from everyday operations, then time to map one task from your own business with the review steps marked.

01

Jobs to billing

The problem

The job is finished, but the labour, materials or customer approval are still on a phone, a clipboard or in someone’s head.

In the seminar

Follow a sample job from work order to invoice review and see which details the office needs at each handoff.

What you can work toward

If this is the task you choose: a map of your own job-to-billing handoff, with the checks for missing details marked.

02

Follow-ups

The problem

Voice notes and conversations pile up. The reply you know how to write still hasn’t been written.

In the seminar

Turn a spoken note into a customer-reply draft or a task list, then check the names, dates and any promises.

What you can work toward

Drafts and task lists you check before using them, with the seminar’s reusable AI prompts and review checklist.

03

Paperwork

The problem

Receipts, invoices and job documents end up in different places, and the gaps show up at review time.

In the seminar

Organize sample receipts and invoices, propose clear file names and flag missing information.

What you can work toward

A sample summary format you can adapt for your accountant’s review. Accounting and tax decisions stay with them.

You map one task in the room. Everyone leaves with the workflow map for that task, reusable AI prompts, a review checklist and a seven-day action plan.

BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Common questions.

Ask your own question ↗
Is this for my kind of business?

The work-tracking examples are especially relevant to construction, trades, equipment service and property maintenance. The email, documents and AI examples suit owners in other sectors too. Bring a recurring task you want to examine.

Do I need to know how to use AI?

We’ll start with the task and walk through the example. The focus is a process you can understand and check.

Will this be a sales presentation?

You’ll work through practical examples and leave with tools you can use. SnapSuite and scoped consulting are optional next steps. You do not need to buy either to use the seminar material.

Will this handle my accounting or file my taxes?

The paperwork examples help organize information and flag gaps for review. They do not replace your accountant, determine tax treatment or file a return.

Will anything be sent or changed in my accounts?

The demonstrations use fictional information and draft outputs. Any real account access, file change or message sending needs its own approval.

Where is it being held?

The planned venue is Trade Winds Hotel, Antigua. The booking is still being finalized, so the venue could change. Register your interest to receive the confirmed venue and booking details before you decide whether to pay.

What does the onsite option include?

Two hours at an agreed location, focused on one agreed workflow, with practical work and process notes. We’ll confirm suitability, access, scope, price and timing before you commit. Only a confirmed booking secures a time.
